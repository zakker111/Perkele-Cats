# 23 — First Episode Progression

## Objective

The first episode should teach every major system naturally through small situations.

## Beat 1 — Learn to click

Player enters a compact scene. A visible object can be inspected and a simple path can be clicked.

## Beat 2 — Learn inventory

Player collects a harmless item and opens the inventory.

## Beat 3 — Learn pipe/beer conflict

Player receives both pipe and beer. The game intentionally creates a situation where the player wants to drink while the pipe is active. The UI teaches the required swap rule.

## Beat 4 — First food

Player finds berries or bread, takes damage or starts slightly injured, then learns healing.

## Beat 5 — First safe puzzle

Puzzle uses 2-3 visible clues and has no active enemy pressure.

## Beat 6 — First cat

A Perkele cat creates a brief hazard. Failure is survivable and funny.

## Beat 7 — First hammer event

An NPC telegraphs a hammer action. Player learns that dangerous situations are short interaction moments rather than conventional combat arenas.

## Beat 8 — Beer distortion

A controlled scene demonstrates the surreal state. At least one harmless visual change and one funny interaction should occur.

## Beat 9 — Seppo

A rare Seppo encounter provides food if the player is badly stocked/injured. The encounter feels lucky and funny, not guaranteed on every playthrough.

## Beat 10 — Finale puzzle

A slightly larger but still safe puzzle combines previously learned observations/items. Completion unlocks the episode ending.

## Pacing target

Introduce one new major rule at a time. Avoid combining three new systems in the same first minute.
