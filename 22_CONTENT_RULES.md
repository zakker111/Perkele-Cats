# 22 — Content and Comedy Rules

## Originality

The game may be inspired by the tone and genre of classic adult comedy adventure games, but it must use original characters, dialogue, visuals, sounds, settings, jokes, puzzle logic, and names.

## Comedy style

Prefer:

- absurd cause and effect;
- deadpan reactions;
- visual gags;
- unexpected but understandable item interactions;
- recurring jokes with variations;
- funny consequences instead of harsh punishment.

Avoid:

- shock-only content with no gameplay purpose;
- cruelty as the primary joke;
- copied catchphrases or recognizable character imitations;
- puzzles that rely on arbitrary moon logic without clues.

## Beer content

Beer is fictional gameplay context. Do not present drinking as medical treatment or a real-world health recommendation. The surreal effects are a game mechanic.

## Soap content

Soap may be used as a ridiculous fictional item, but do not provide real-world encouragement to consume soap or describe it as safe.

## Language

Strong language may be used as character flavor where appropriate, but it should remain purposeful. Keep key instructions readable and unambiguous.

## NPC diversity

Do not make an entire category of person the punchline. Give characters individual quirks and motives.
